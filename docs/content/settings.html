Open Settings from the sidebar (or g then s). It is one scrolling page with anchored sections: a side list follows the section in view from 900 pixels wide, and a row of pill links does the same on narrower screens. Each section has its own address, for example /settings#calendar.

Template and sections

Template and sections. (1) Rename, hide and reorder, (2) quick actions switch.
  • Template. Shows the name and description of this journal's template. It cannot be changed here; make a new journal for another one. New journal… and Request a template are here too.
  • Sections. One row per section. Edit the name (up to 30 characters; it saves when you press Enter or click away; emptying it restores the template's name). Untick Show to hide a section from the menus (Today and Journal cannot be hidden). Use ↑ and ↓ to reorder.
  • Show quick actions above the note box. Appears only for templates that have quick actions (Stardew Valley).
  • Extra sections. Buttons such as Add “tasks” create a suggested tag and pin it. A pinned tag appears in the sidebar as a shortcut.
  • Reset sections to the template's defaults. Undoes your names, hiding, order and quick-action choice.

Hiding a section only removes it from navigation. Its records are still there, and hidden pages still open by address.

Where each setting is kept

SettingKept
Calendar, sections, quick actions, marker types, reading size, the "hide this tip" choiceIn the journal (so they travel in an export and differ per journal)
Theme, single-key shortcuts, map View choices and the style for new map drawingsIn the browser (the same for every journal, not exported)

The journal's settings are written into Export JSON and restored by an import. See Export format.
