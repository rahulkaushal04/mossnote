--- title: Stardew Valley template description: The Farm section, the calendar of seasons, NPCs and the four quick actions that the Stardew Valley template adds. ---

The Stardew Valley template is the Default template plus a Farm section, a calendar of four seasons, four quick actions under the note box, and different names for two sections. Its tint is plum. Like every template it contains no game information: the seasons are just names you can change, and every crop, villager and place in your journal is something you typed.

The calendar of seasons

Dates look like Spring 3, or Spring 3 · Year 2 when the year matters. Set today's date with the picker (how); moving to the next day rolls Summer 28 over to Fall 1 and Winter 28 into Spring 1 of the next year. Rename seasons or change their lengths in Settings → Calendar.

Quick actions

Four pill buttons sit under the note box. Each one sets a flag or tag on the note you are writing, so a note taken in the middle of play takes one tap instead of several.

After choosing Met someone: (1) the button shows it is on, (2) the #met tag has been added to the note.
  • Tap a button again to turn it off. Several can be on at once. We confirmed that the flag buttons toggle the flags and that Met someone and To do add and remove their tags.
  • The tags are created the first time you use them. The buttons never create anything on their own.
  • To hide the row, untick Show quick actions above the note box in Settings → Template and sections.

NPCs

This is the People section under another name. Use Track progress on an NPC's page to count anything you like with a row of dots, such as friendship steps. Mossnote does not know how many steps a game has: you set the maximum, from 1 to 99.

The Farm

The Farm lists what you have planted. An entry is "a crop" in this template's words, but you can record anything that has a planted date and a harvested date.

The Farm. (1) Add an entry, (2) growing, (3) done.
  • Add an entry by typing a name (up to 80 characters) in Add an entry… and pressing Enter. Names you used before are suggested as you type. If an in-game date is set, the entry starts as planted on that date.
  • Entries are grouped. Growing has a planted date and no harvest. Done has a harvest date. Noted has neither (you recorded it but have not planted it, or no date was set). Each group shows a count.
  • Each row shows its name, then its dates (for example Spring 3 → Spring 7, with the year added when it differs from the current year) and counts (planted 20, harvested 15), and its tags on the right.

Harvesting and planting again

Point at (or focus) a row to reveal its quick action. On a touch screen the action is always shown.

  1. On a Growing entry choose Harvested today. It records the current in-game date as the harvest date. (With no date set, a date picker opens instead.)
  2. A Harvested count box opens. Type a whole number from 0 to 999,999 and press Enter or Save, or press Esc to skip it.
  3. The entry moves to Done. A harvest date earlier than the planting date is refused: Harvest can't be before planting.
Entering the harvested count. (1) The count, (2) Save.

On a Done entry, Plant again creates a new entry with the same name and tags, planted on the current in-game date, so you can track the next round.

An entry's page

Choose an entry to open its page. On a wide screen it slides in from the right over the list; on a narrow screen it is a full page. It has its own address, closes with Esc or Back, and can be bookmarked.

On Today

Up to three Growing entries appear on Today with their planted date, and a link to the Farm.

Suggested tags and sections

The template suggests two tags, tasks and custom notes. Under Settings → Template and sections → Extra sections, a click creates the tag and pins it, so it shows up in the sidebar under Pinned. Neither tag exists until you click.

Things you might expect that are not there

  • No game data. There is no crop list, villager list, gift guide or calendar of events. Mossnote will not suggest one; the project excludes it on purpose.
  • No automatic harvest times. Growing is a label, not a timer.
  • No completion tracking, statistics or checklists. These are deliberately out of scope.
